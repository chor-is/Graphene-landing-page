Graphene Powder

HiPER Multilayer Graphene

Product type:ประเภทผลิตภัณฑ์: Multilayer Graphene

A light, fluffy black powder of 5–15 stacked graphene layers, made from biomass-derived carbon. Use it as an additive in polymers, rubber, coatings, inks, cement and conductive composites, or as the base for your own graphene products.

Graphene FIR Emission Screen Printing Ink for Textiles

Water-based Screen Printing Ink

The body gives off heat as infrared. Graphene printed on fabric absorbs that heat and re-emits it as far-infrared (FIR) at 4–14 μm, part of which goes back into the body as gentle warmth.

  • Far-infrared emissionปล่อยรังสีฟาร์อินฟราเรดBoosts FIR at 4–14 μm.เพิ่มการปล่อยรังสี FIR ช่วง 4–14 μm
  • Comfortable warmthมอบความอบอุ่นสบายSupports circulation, reduces fatigue.ช่วยการไหลเวียนโลหิต ลดความเมื่อยล้า
